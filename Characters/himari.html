Biography

Himari Nightingale, a 4 year old part-cat-kitsune-dragon human hybrid girl and Ash's youngest daughter. Himari inherits a lot of traits and abilities from her parents, having pink cat ears, several fox tails and a pair of small feathery black wings, as well as a grand amount of latent magical potential, though she currently has very limited access to her innate magic. Himari uses her “Umbral Tempest” deck to duel.
