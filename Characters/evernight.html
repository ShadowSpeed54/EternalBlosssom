Biography

Evernight, a pure child of Anāsrava, born from the Aeon of Remembrance, Fuli, and the alter ego of March 7th. Her true origins and identity are unknown, simply going by the name “Evernight”. Evernight wields an umbrella that is sharp enough to be used as a weapon in combat. She is a duo-emanator of Remembrance and Enigmata, having both the powers of cryokinesis and the power of mnemokinesis. Evernight can summon constructs of Remembrance, in the form of jellyfish, which also share her own power.
