Biography

Lily Blossom, a 49 year old half-cat woman and the mother of Ash and Rose. Lily possesses both cat traits and innate magical potential, being born with a pair of pink cat ears and a tail, as well as a high level of mastery over magic. Lily uses her “Ultima Arcane Arts” deck to duel.
