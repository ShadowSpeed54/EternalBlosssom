Biography

Alicia Celestrixia is a 20-year-old young woman from Lymerose, the capital city of Britannia Nova, and the younger sister of Astrid Celestrixia.

In her day-to-day life, Alicia works as a nursing assistant at Lymerose Central Hospital in the Axiom Gardens, providing care and support to patients in need, and after hours, she works on writing short stories for her online community.

In combat, she dual-wields her scythe, named "Geosplitter" after its ease of use in slicing through rocks and minerals, in tandem with her Duel Disk, affectionately called "Cyberse Loader", at once, utilising the speed and grace of the scythe together with her "Cynet Link" deck to become an overpowering force in combat.
