Biography

Alicia Celestrixia is a 21-year-old young woman from Lymerose, the capital city of Britannia Nova, and the younger sister of Astrid Celestrixia.

Alicia, much like her older sister, is a highly intelligent and creative individual. She is known for her sharp wit and quick thinking, often using her intelligence to solve complex problems. She works as a nurse in Lymerose Central Hospital, often spending most of her time in her designated ward than at home.

In combat, Alicia wields Cynetshift, a blue-white armoured twofold Duel Disk and razor-sharp scythe capable of slicing through even the toughest materials. The weapon is capable of transforming – it can shift into Assault Form by detaching itself from Alicia's arm, removing the guard covering its blade and extending its handle, and it can shift back into Strategy Mode by collapsing the handle and closing the blade guard, Alicia manually placing it onto her arm herself.

Abilities

  • Manifestation: With Cynetshift in Strategy Mode, the world in front of Alicia becomes her personal Duel Monsters field. She can summon and discard monsters and set and activate cards at will, manifesting as a real presence, like how it's portrayed in the Yu-Gi-Oh anime adaptations.
  • Enchantment: With Cynetshift in Assault Mode, monsters that Alicia has summoned and other cards that Alicia has set in Strategy Mode will change affinity and imbue Cynetshift itself with power. Monster cards act as though the monster is attacking during Alicia's scythe swings, Spell cards affinitise her offence and Trap cards affinitise her defence.
