Biography

March 7th, a member of the Astral Express and a pure child of Anāsrava, born from the Aeon of Remembrance, Fuli. Her true origins are completely unknown, even to herself, after losing her past memories. March is an emanator of Remembrance, having the ability of cryokinesis, through her Six-Phased Ice. March also can summon constructs of Remembrance, in the form of jellyfish, that can detonate themselves. She wields a bow, her weapon's capabilities amplified by her Six-Phased-Ice. March's power of Remembrance allows her to manipulate and access memories although her alter ego, Evernight, has a much better mastery over these abilities.
